Teacher labels
NetMHCpan 4.1

Every overlapping PDA 9-mer labeled for EL rank, BA rank, and BA affinity. The student imitates the teacher in designed-protein space — it does not beat NetMHCpan on NetMHCpan’s own target.

Representation
Frozen ESM-2 650M

Mean of nine residue vectors. Compact head: layer-norm → GELU + dropout → three sigmoid outputs. Deployment averages the fold heads.

Inherited separately
Chao1 cleavage · TAP

Processing heads stay as separate evidence. They are not jointly trained with the PDA MHC student and have not been revalidated as the MHC headline result.

Corpus: Protein Design Archive designed parents — not the PDB. Labels are NetMHCpan 4.1 for HLA-A*02:01. Agreement with the teacher is not experimental presentation accuracy; a separate non-PDA cohort is required for biological validation.
Reading the score

Named fields, not one score.
Three decompositions you can challenge.

1 · Named outputs
Separate fields — not one immunogenicity score.

overall_mhci_risk is EL presentation propensity. composite_processing_risk (Ri) is the geometric mean of N-cleavage, C-cleavage, and BA. Campaign ranking uses pathway score Si = EL0.70·(√(N·C))0.30; the protein summary is the mean of the top-5 Si. Unitless triage heuristics — not immune-response probabilities.

2 · The confidence score
Four things we can measure.

Not a coverage probability. Four observable quantities, geometrically averaged, so a low one cannot be hidden by three high ones.

σTAP0.76how tightly the 50 bootstrap ridges agree

Confidence0.61 The heads disagree on this window. The score says so instead of hiding it.

The guard rails
  • ≤ 3 substitutions, ≤ 6 steps, 8 ESM-2 mask-fill candidates per step
  • Protected positions — hotspot-contacting residues are never masked
  • Preservation gate — reject if ESM log-likelihood drops > 0.5 per residue
  • No cleavage/TAP regression — penalised in the objective, not ignored
✓ Accepted on the re-embedded discrete sequence — latent distance only ranks candidates; it never decides.
Animated illustration of the objective implemented in src/re_agent/e2e_pls/steer.py, not a recorded run. The latent plane is a 2-D sketch of the 64-d MHC projection. Every accepted mutation in the real loop is re-encoded and re-scored on the discrete sequence before acceptance, and the trace is written to JSON with the model version, dataset hash, and every rejected candidate and its reason.
