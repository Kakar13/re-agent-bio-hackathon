They never ask whether it was ever made.

The idea

Antigen processing, rewritten as
three projections of one vector.

cleavage 2560→128→64→2, BCE + isotonic TAP ridge α=10, 50-sample bootstrap σ MHC PCA-64 + per-allele binder centroid risk = geometric mean, TAP reported beside it
Scores shown are an illustrative window. TAP is deliberately excluded from the composite: at 613 measured peptides it is a regularized ridge with bootstrap uncertainty, not a calibrated probability, so it is reported alongside the risk rather than multiplied into it. Confidence is a decomposed 0–1 diagnostic, not a coverage probability.
What taught it · and what it already shows

Five million labelled windows —
and de novo proteins already stand out.

Cleavage labels
Pepsickle

Run once per parent protein, not per peptide — it predicts a cleavage probability at every residue, and we slice per window. Pinned to torch 1.13, so it lives in its own venv and is called as a subprocess.

Presentation labels
MHCflurry 2

Affinity in nM and allele percentile for all 5,000,000 teacher rows. Binders (≤2nd percentile) define the centroid the MHC head measures cosine distance to.

Transport labels
613 measured peptides

DS613 is the whole world of public TAP measurements. We impute the rest with a ridge on one-hot 9-mers and flag every imputed row — the TAP head is only ever fit on the 613 real ones.

Reading the score

One number is a rumour.
Three decompositions are an argument.

1 · The risk score
Why a product, not an average.

Presentation needs every gate to open. An average forgives a closed one; a geometric mean does not — one near-zero gate drags the whole score to zero, which is what biology does.

cleave N0.91
cleave C0.88
presentation0.97
³√( 0.91 × 0.88 × 0.97 ) 0.92
TAP −0.42 ± 0.31 reported beside the risk, never multiplied into it — 613 measured peptides is a ridge with error bars, not a calibrated probability
2 · The confidence score
Four things we can measure.

Not a coverage probability. Four observable quantities, geometrically averaged, so a low one cannot be hidden by three high ones.

σTAP0.76how tightly the 50 bootstrap ridges agree
margin0.94how far presentation sits from the 0.5 boundary
context1.00fraction of the ±4 aa flanks actually present
agreement0.31how tightly the three heads cluster — they don't, here
Confidence0.61 The heads disagree on this window. The score says so instead of hiding it.
3 · Explainability
We do not show you attention.

The heads are an MLP, a ridge, and a cosine — there are no attention maps to show, and borrowing ESM's would explain the encoder, not the prediction. So we decompose the prediction itself onto residues.

risk_max · worst 9-mer covering each residue
mhc_importance · residue cosine to the binder centroid
Worst window · 34–43 Nine windows cover each residue; each is painted by the worst one. The second track is the same cosine that Steer to Safety picks its mutation site from — so the picture and the decision are the same quantity.
Both tracks come from score.compute_residue_heatmap and score.attach_mhc_residue_importance; values shown are illustrative for one binder region. risk_max is the maximum composite risk over the ≤9 windows covering a residue, carrying the confidence of that window. mhc_importance maps residue-level cosine from [−1,1] to [0,1] so it shares the risk colour scale — it is an alignment score, not an attribution with a conservation guarantee.

The guard rails
  • ≤ 3 substitutions, ≤ 6 steps, 8 ESM-2 mask-fill candidates per step
  • Protected positions — hotspot-contacting residues are never masked
  • Preservation gate — reject if ESM log-likelihood drops > 0.5 per residue
  • No cleavage/TAP regression — penalised in the objective, not ignored
✓ Accepted on the re-embedded discrete sequence — latent distance only ranks candidates; it never decides.
Animated illustration of the objective implemented in src/re_agent/e2e_pls/steer.py, not a recorded run. The latent plane is a 2-D sketch of the 64-d MHC projection. Every accepted mutation in the real loop is re-encoded and re-scored on the discrete sequence before acceptance, and the trace is written to JSON with the model version, dataset hash, and every rejected candidate and its reason.

The honest part

A surrogate that says so.
The failure mode of this field is confident silence.

What this is not
Not immunogenicity.
  • Labels are teacher predictions from Pepsickle and MHCflurry, not measured T-cell responses.
  • The composite treats three gates as independent. Biology does not.
  • One allele: HLA-A*02:01. A real population needs a panel.
  • MHC-II and CD4 help are entirely absent.
Every score we emit carries this disclaimer string in the JSON trace, not just on a slide.
What is actually load-bearing
The parts we can defend.
  • Cluster-grouped splits with a hard leakage gate — 0 clusters span two splits.
  • Isotonic calibration on held-out data, stored as breakpoints, so a checkpoint is diffable.
  • Decomposed confidence: bootstrap σ, decision margin, flank completeness, head agreement.
  • Discrete verification — steering never accepts a sequence it has not re-scored.
No pickled sklearn or torch objects anywhere in a checkpoint. Plain arrays plus JSON.
