Where the thinking lives

A thought either becomes a word,
or it stays in the hidden layers.

Every other LLM · explicit CoT 0reasoning tokens
3 reasoning steps · 27 tokens decoded, emitted, and read back in

Every step leaves the network, is decoded into words, and is fed back in as input. You are billed for each one.

InnerThink · latent CoT 0reasoning tokens
3 reasoning steps · 3 forward passes, 0 tokens decoded

The last hidden layer loops straight back to the first. The forward passes still cost compute — they just never cost a token.

Five layers stand in for 36 and three reasoning steps for six: CODI-Qwen3-8B runs six latent steps through the full transformer stack, with the recurrence being the last hidden state projected through a 2-layer MLP + LayerNorm. Counts are reasoning tokens only, at ~9 per illustrated step — both sides still emit the answer, and a latent step still costs a forward pass. What it does not cost is a token.
How it learns

The words teach the vectors.
Then the words are thrown away.

Self-distillation: one network runs both tasks. The student never copies the teacher's words — it copies what those words did to the network inside.

LoRA r=128, α 32 on q,k,v,o,gate,up,down 0.35 B trained / 8.2 B frozen ≈388k GSM8k-Aug-NL + CommonsenseQA 5 epochs · 8× H100 · lr 2e-4
